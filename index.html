About Me

I build deployed AI systems and conduct independent reinforcement learning research. My work spans computer vision for retail loss prevention (Sentra Retail OS, co-founded), edge AI on hybrid Radxa/Hailo + Vast.ai infrastructure, and world-model failure modes in model-based RL (DreamerV3 research, targeting RLC 2026). I care about edge AI, real-world deployment, and democratizing AI access in Central Asia.

Research

  • "When the World Model Lies" — DreamerV3 Failure Modes: Independent research on world-model hallucination and value-function divergence in model-based RL. Findings include imagined-to-real reward ratios reaching ~50× on AntMaze at 500k steps and KL divergence collapse as a leading indicator (r=−0.91, ~50k step lag). Published on Zenodo with reproducible HuggingFace Space and GitHub repository. Targeting arXiv submission and RLC 2026.
  • Polygence Research Fellow (2025): Selected with partial-tuition scholarship (~40% acceptance); conducted mentored ML research project.
  • JOAI 2026 — Neural Decoding: Placed 27/98 in international competition predicting mouse lever position from calcium imaging data.
